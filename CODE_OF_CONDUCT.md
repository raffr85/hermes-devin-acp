# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, constructive, and considerate. Focus criticism on ideas and
code, accept feedback gracefully, and respect privacy. Harassment, threats,
discrimination, sexualized attention, trolling, and publishing others' private
information are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove or reject comments, commits, issues, and other
contributions that violate these standards and may temporarily or permanently
ban abusive participants. Report incidents privately through the repository's
security reporting channel. Maintainers will protect reporters' privacy where
possible and apply consequences fairly.

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project in
public spaces.
